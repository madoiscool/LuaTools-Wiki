# How to Apply Voices38 Cracks

:::tip Before You Continue
If your game is already installed: [Click Here](voices38/revert)
:::

**Step 1**: Go to Fixes and look for your game.

**Step 2**: Click on 'Manifest'.

**Step 3**: Download the game.

**Step 4**: After the game is downloaded, Click on 'Fix'.

**Step 5**: In the Steam Library, Right Click the game -> 'Browse Local Files' and launch the game's exe directly.
